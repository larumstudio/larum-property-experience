# LARUM PROPERTY EXPERIENCE™
## V2 scope — intelligent property experience

**Status:** Backlog / not started  
**Depends on:** V1 experience, authorised property data, real lead destination and analytics consent.

## 1. V2 objective

Turn the Property Experience from an immersive presentation into an intelligent property advisory layer that understands the visitor, the property and the next commercial step.

## 2. Candidate V2 capabilities

### Property Concierge advanced

- deeper property and location knowledge;
- source-aware answers;
- answers linked to scenes, facts and documents;
- bilingual conversational memory;
- uncertainty handling;
- advisor handoff.

### Buyer intent

- progressive profiling;
- purpose: main residence, second residence, investment;
- priorities: privacy, family, architecture, entertaining, wellness, city, landscape;
- timing and readiness;
- soft budget qualification;
- lead summary.

### Personalised experience

- reorder emphasis based on declared interests;
- prepare a “for you” property route;
- generate a private property summary;
- recommend relevant documents and scenes.

### Agent intelligence

- conversation summary;
- buyer questions;
- visited scenes;
- declared priorities;
- suggested follow-up;
- lead status.

### Analytics

- chapter progression;
- scenes opened;
- videos watched;
- downloads requested;
- calculator interaction;
- Concierge questions;
- enquiry conversion;
- privacy/consent compliant reporting.

### Private buyer room

- gated documentation;
- additional plans and technical data;
- valuation/market material;
- follow-up conversation;
- invitation-only access.

### AI lifestyle simulation

Only after a verified property content pack exists. Any generated scenario must be clearly labelled as interpretive, not a photograph or promise of the actual property.

## 3. Explicitly not first priority

- avatar;
- voice;
- fully automated negotiations;
- autonomous investment advice;
- unverified market claims;
- automated generation without human approval.

## 4. V2 success

V2 succeeds if the experience can recognise what a visitor cares about, guide them to the most relevant property evidence and deliver a useful, qualified summary to the human advisor without making the experience feel like a form or a CRM system.
