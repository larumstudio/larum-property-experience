## 2. What must be completed for V1

### A. Final experience layer

- coherent opening sequence;
- final property thesis;
- property-specific narrative;
- final scene order;
- transitions that feel cinematic, not decorative;
- mobile equivalent;
- final CTA rhythm.

### B. Media layer

- hero loop or motion;
- property film;
- micro-videos;
- final posters and fallbacks;
- plans;
- downloadable PDFs;
- authorised, unwatermarked assets.

### C. Property intelligence layer

- verified property data;
- verified location data;
- acquisition-cost assumptions reviewed;
- weaknesses/limitations handled honestly;
- document version/date information.

### D. Concierge layer

- complete property knowledge pack;
- surroundings knowledge pack;
- bilingual answers;
- links from answers to scenes;
- uncertainty/fallback rules;
- advisor handoff;
- lead summary.

### E. Commercial/technical layer

- secure form endpoint;
- email/WhatsApp destination;
- consent/privacy text;
- event analytics;
- performance audit;
- cross-browser/mobile QA;
- final publication strategy.

## 3. What is not necessary before demonstrating the product

Do not delay the first showcase for:

- avatar;
- voice concierge;
- CRM;
- buyer accounts;
- 3D tour;
- full personalisation;
- AI-generated lifestyle simulation;
- automated generation of every property;
- advanced scarcity engine;
- predictive learning system.

## 4. Definition of V1 completion

V1 is complete when a qualified visitor can:

1. enter a property-specific experience;
2. understand its identity;
3. experience how it changes through the day;
4. explore relationships between spaces;
5. watch or access original video;
6. inspect facts and documents;
7. estimate acquisition costs with editable assumptions;
8. ask a credible Property Concierge;
9. request a private conversation;
10. use the experience comfortably in EN/ES and on mobile.

## 5. Current conclusion

The product skeleton is substantially built. The largest remaining gap is not another feature. It is the final combination of:

```text
real authorised assets
+
property-specific creative direction
+
polished transitions
+
verified intelligence
+
credible concierge
+
commercial QA
```

That is the work that turns the current prototype into the showcase promised by the original vision.
